Header Verdict

Paste the headers of a delivered message. The first line you read is what failed and why.

Nothing you paste leaves your browser. No server, no log.
Reads Authentication-Results (RFC 8601), ARC (RFC 8617), DKIM-Signature (RFC 6376), and Received (RFC 5321) as the receiving servers wrote them. It does not query DNS or verify signatures, so alignment uses relaxed mode unless the receiver reported otherwise, and the organizational domain comes from an abbreviated public suffix list.
